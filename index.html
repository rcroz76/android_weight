📋 Nutrition & Target Formulas Explained

🔥
Calorie Deficit Formula

(Body Weight in lbs × 12) - 500 kcal provides a steady fat-loss deficit of approximately 1 lb (0.45 kg) per week while fueling daily energy and training sessions.

🥩
Protein Intake Range (0.7g – 1.0g / lb)

Consuming 0.7g to 1.0g of protein per pound of body weight maximizes muscle protein synthesis (MPS) and protects lean muscle mass during weight loss.

📊
BMI Metric & Height

Body Mass Index evaluates body weight relative to your height in meters (kg / m²). Use the "Edit Height" button above to keep your height accurate.
